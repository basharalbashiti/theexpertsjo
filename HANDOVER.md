# The Experts - Website Admin Guide

Welcome to your website admin guide. This document explains how to manage your website at **theexpertsjo.com** without needing any technical knowledge.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Go to **https://theexpertsjo.com/admin**
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click **Sign In**

Default login:
- Username: **admin**
- Password: **admin123**

**Important: Change your password immediately after your first login** (see "Changing Your Password" below).

### Changing Your Password

1. Log in to the admin panel
2. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password (minimum 6 characters)
5. Confirm your new password
6. Click **Change Password**

## What You Can Manage

Everything on the website can be updated from the admin panel — no coding required.

### Site Content (Text & Translations)

Edit all text on the website in both English and Arabic.

- Click **Site Content** in the sidebar
- Find the text you want to change
- Edit the English and/or Arabic versions
- Click **Save**

### Site Images

Upload and change images across the website.

- Click **Site Images** in the sidebar
- Sections include:
  - **Site Logo** — the logo in the navigation bar and footer
  - **Favicon** — the small icon shown in browser tabs
  - **Social Share Image** — the image shown when someone shares your website link on social media
  - **Hero Banners** — the large sliding images on the homepage
  - **Page Banners** — banner images for Products, Contact, About, and Partners pages
- Click **Upload Photo** to replace any image
- Click the trash icon to remove a custom image (reverts to default)

### Categories

Manage your product categories (e.g., Room Service, Linens, Spa, Amenities).

- Click **Categories** in the sidebar
- Add new categories with English and Arabic names
- Upload a category image
- Set the display order
- Toggle visibility to show/hide categories on the website

### Products

Manage products within each category.

- Click **Products** in the sidebar
- Add products with names, descriptions, and images in both languages
- Each product can have up to 4 additional gallery images
- Assign products to categories

### Our Brands

Manage the brand logos displayed on the homepage slider.

- Click **Our Brands** in the sidebar
- Add, remove, or reorder brand logos

### Partners & Clients

Manage your business partners and hotel clients.

- Click **Partners & Clients** in the sidebar
- **Partners tab** — brand partners (e.g., Abdeen, Cashmere)
- **Clients tab** — hotel brand clients (e.g., Four Seasons, Marriott)
- Upload logos and set display order for each

### Leadership

Manage the leadership team shown on the About page.

- Click **Leadership** in the sidebar
- Add team members with name, role, photo, phone, and email
- Both English and Arabic versions supported
- Toggle visibility to show/hide members

### Key Contacts

Manage the key contact persons shown on the Contact page.

- Click **Key Contacts** in the sidebar
- Works the same way as Leadership

### Quick Links

Manage the links shown in the website footer.

- Click **Quick Links** in the sidebar
- Add, edit, or remove footer navigation links
- Toggle visibility for each link

### Messages

View messages submitted through the contact form on the website.

- Click **Messages** in the sidebar
- View, read, and delete contact form submissions
- Messages are also sent to **alaa@theexpertsjo.com** by email

### SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

Control how your website appears on Google and social media.

- Click **SEO** in the sidebar
- Edit page title, meta description, and social media preview text
- All fields support both English and Arabic
- Manage sitemap URL and robots.txt settings

## Tips

- **Images**: For best results, use high-quality images. Recommended sizes:
  - Hero banners: 1920 x 800 pixels
  - Category images: 800 x 600 pixels
  - Logos/brands: 400 x 200 pixels (transparent PNG)
  - Favicon: 64 x 64 or 128 x 128 pixels (PNG)
  - Social share image: 1200 x 630 pixels (PNG)
- **Order**: Use the sort order field to control the display order of items
- **Visibility**: Use the visibility toggle to hide items without deleting them
- **Save often**: Changes are saved immediately when you click Save or Upload
